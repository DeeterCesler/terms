Policy Checker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 18, 2026

Policy Checker is a Chrome extension that shows you a privacy summary for whatever website you are currently visiting. This page describes exactly what the extension does with your data.

What the extension reads

  • The domain of the tab you are currently viewing (for example, example.com) — only when you click the extension icon. The extension does not read the full URL, page contents, form inputs, cookies, or anything else.

What the extension sends

  • The domain is sent to our analysis server at terms-vzh0.onrender.com over HTTPS so we can return the saved privacy analysis for that site.
  • If you press the Request Analysis button on a site we have not yet covered, that domain is added to our analysis queue. No other information is included.

What we store

  • On our server: the list of domains that have been requested or analyzed, and the public privacy-policy text we fetched for each. We do not store your IP, account, browsing history, or any identifier that ties a request to you.
  • In the extension itself: a short-lived in-memory cache (session storage) of recent analyses, so opening the popup on the same site twice does not re-hit the server. This cache is cleared when Chrome closes.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no email collection.
  • No cookies set by the extension.
  • No third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of data with anyone.
  • No remote code: the extension ships with all its code bundled.

Permissions, explained

  • activeTab — lets the extension read the domain of the tab you are on when you click the icon. Without this, the extension cannot know which site to look up.
  • storage — used for the short-lived in-memory cache described above.
  • host_permissions: https://terms-vzh0.onrender.com/* — lets the extension talk to our analysis server.

Contact

Questions, requests, or want a domain removed from our analysis queue? Email me@deetercesler.com.

This policy applies only to the Policy Checker browser extension and its companion server. It does not cover the privacy policies of the third-party websites Policy Checker analyzes.
